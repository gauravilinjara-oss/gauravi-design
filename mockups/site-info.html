How I built this

No template, no page-builder — every page here is hand-written, one file at a time. Here's the system underneath it, and how the parts that move were made.

01 — the stack

Plain files, on purpose.

02 — the design system

One file is the source of truth.

Every colour, size, space, radius and easing comes from a single tokens.css. It's deliberately small: one cool neutral ramp, one accent, and tints derived from it — never a pile of one-off colours. Change it there, it changes everywhere.

03 — the homepage sky

A sky that knows what time it is.

The landing page isn't a photo or a video — it's divs and gradients. The whole sky is drawn in CSS and quietly retinted to match the actual hour where you're standing. Here it is in miniature — flip it:

a working miniature of the real hero — same gradient stops, same drift.
1

The gradient. Four CSS colour-stops (--sky1…4) from deep blue up to pale horizon. That's the entire sky — one linear-gradient.

2

Time of day. A small script reads your clock and swaps the four stops — morning, noon, golden hour, night — so the sky is honest about the hour. You can also set it yourself with the day/dusk/night/auto toggle.

3

The clouds. Blurred white circles (a div with two pseudo-elements) drifting on a @keyframes loop. At night they turn slate-grey so the hero text stays legible.

4

The life. The sun becomes a moon, stars fade in, and the daytime bees become fireflies. Hand-drawn SVG, flapping on a 0.12s wing loop. No canvas, no WebGL, no library — just the browser.

04 — from the ground up

How it came together.

Start with the words, not the layout.

Every page began as a sentence I was trying to say. The design came after, in service of it.

One tokens file, before anything else.

Colours, type, spacing, motion — decided once, in tokens.css, so nothing drifts. Consistency is a system, not willpower.

Hand-write it, keep it flat.

Plain HTML & CSS a browser reads directly. No framework to outgrow, no build to break. Motion is CSS first, GSAP only where scroll needs choreography.

Make one thing sing per page.

The sky here, a drag-to-reveal painting there. One idea, obsessed over, beats ten that are fine.

made with a machine, on purpose

I design for AI for a living. It felt right to build this with one.

Every page was coded side-by-side with Claude. I brought the taste and the stubbornness; it brought the patience for the hundredth revision. The ideas, the words, and the final call on every pixel are mine.
