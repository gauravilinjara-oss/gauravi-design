the fogged glass

Remember scribbling your name on a misted bathroom mirror? This turns a webcam into fogged glass you write on with your bare hands in the air, hand-tracking and breath detection, running entirely in the browser, nothing leaves your device.

  • ✶ pinch thumb + index to write
  • ✶ make a fist to wipe it clear
  • ✶ hold up two fingers to fog it back up
