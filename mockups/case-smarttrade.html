the bet

A separate tool per product ships faster, but it rebuilds the old silos and makes users relearn the bank product by product. Instead: one hub, one pattern, the whole portfolio in one place.

reframe paper as a flow

A service blueprint of the journey showed the blocker wasn't the form. It was the missing visibility and second-eyes governance once paper left the building.

prototype the online request

LC and guarantee flows with reusable clause templates, digital upload and a maker-checker route built in, not bolted on.

validate with real users

Usability-tested the online flow against the paper process. Far faster, with far more confidence a request was moving.

ship a responsive system

Delivered a responsive design system so the same trade patterns held across products and devices, and new instruments could be added without redrawing the hub.

05 · Solution

the trade hub, made legible.

An import LC is one of the most field-heavy instruments in banking. I designed it as a nine-step tabbed canvas that ends in one scannable review before anything is committed.

07 · Design decisions

controls kept, paper dropped.

The hard part of digitising a regulated instrument is keeping its governance intact while shedding its paper. Four decisions carried that weight.

01

Discrepancies, handled in-flow

When a discrepant bill arrives, the user sees settlement, forex and charges together and accepts or rejects on one screen. The moment that once meant a phone call and days of limbo.

02

Three ways to settle

At settlement the user picks how to fund it (Trust Receipt, account debit, or account + loan) and links forex deals inside the flow. Financing is a choice, not a separate trip to the bank.

03

One structure, every action

Create, amend, view and settle all share one tabbed anatomy. The Figma file's own note: "all the other tabs follow the same structure as LC creation." Learn it once, use it everywhere.

04

Review before you commit

The Review step has expanded, collapsed and error states, so nothing submits half-finished, then routes through maker-checker for a real second pair of eyes.
